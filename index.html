SWIFT Fee Payment Identifier (SPI)

The SWIFT Fee Payment Identifier (SPI) is an administrative identifier used to identify and reconcile a processing fee associated with an international transfer request. The applicable fee must be recorded and verified by the authorized payment administrator before an SPI is issued and linked to the relevant transfer request. The SPI is separate from the six-digit SWIFT verification code. For assistance, contact the support team or the authorized payment administrator.
