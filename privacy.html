Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026 · Last updated: October 23, 2026

Demonstration environment. This website is not a licensed bank and is not intended for real financial activity. Please do not enter real bank credentials, payment-card details, government ID numbers, or other highly sensitive personal or financial information.

1. Scope of this policy

This policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and managed when you visit or create a demonstration account on the Novexxa Bank website (the “Service”). It describes the current intended operation of the demo; actual processing also depends on the site's configuration and the service providers it uses.

2. Information you may provide

Depending on which features you use, the Service may process:

  • Profile information: legal first and last name, optional middle name or salutation, and username or profile details where requested.
  • Contact information: email address, phone number, country or region, preferred display currency, and communication preference.
  • Account setup information: selected demo account type and purpose, password-authentication data, and a transaction PIN used for simulated protected workflows.
  • Service records: demo account/profile records, simulated transfer or withdrawal requests, support messages, verification state, and related administrative records.
  • Technical information: information that may be generated by the hosting, authentication, database, or security services, such as timestamps, request logs, and diagnostic events, according to their configuration.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to create and authenticate a demo account, support email confirmation, display profile settings, run simulated workflows, respond to support requests, maintain and secure the website, troubleshoot errors, and test features. The Service should not be used to make real financial decisions or to submit information that is not needed for a demonstration.

4. Authentication and transaction PIN

Sign-in credentials are handled through Supabase Authentication. The application is designed to send the transaction PIN to a server-side database function for hashing rather than intentionally storing it as plain text in the public profile record. This description is not a guarantee that the overall application has undergone an independent security audit. Do not reuse a password or PIN used for any real account.

5. Service providers and storage

The project uses Supabase for authentication and database services. Information submitted to the website may be processed and stored within the project's configured Supabase infrastructure. Hosting, email delivery, and other technical providers may process limited information as needed to provide their services. Provider processing is also subject to the relevant provider's policies and the project's configuration.

6. Access and disclosure

Authorized project administrators may access stored demo records to maintain the application, troubleshoot issues, review support requests, and test administrative workflows. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect the Service, its users, or the rights and safety of others. The demo is not designed to process real banking information.

7. Retention and deletion

Demo information may remain in the database until it is deleted by an authorized administrator or removed as part of maintenance. The current interface may not provide a self-service account or data deletion control. To ask about a record or request review or deletion, email support@facil-hub.online from the address associated with your demo account where practical. We may need to verify the request before taking action, and applicable technical or legal constraints may affect the response.

8. Security

The project uses technical controls intended to limit access to data, including Supabase authentication and database access policies. No website or storage system can be guaranteed perfectly secure, and this demonstration has not been represented as meeting production banking security or regulatory requirements. Avoid submitting real financial or identity information.

9. Your choices

You can choose not to register or to stop using the demo. Required registration fields are identified in the form. If you do not provide required information, some features may not work. Where the website records acceptance of the Terms and acknowledgement of this Policy, that record is used to document the registration step.

10. Children

The Service is intended for general product demonstration, not for collecting children's personal information. Do not submit personal information about a child through the demo.

11. International processing

Because the project uses online infrastructure providers, submitted information may be processed in locations different from your own, depending on the provider configuration. Local privacy laws may give you specific rights concerning access, correction, deletion, or other processing. The applicable rights and obligations depend on your circumstances and relevant law.

12. Updates to this policy

This policy may be updated as the demonstration changes. The date at the top shows the latest stated revision. Please review this page periodically if you continue using the demo.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests about this demonstration, email support@facil-hub.online. This is a project support contact, not a regulated bank's privacy office or complaints service.
